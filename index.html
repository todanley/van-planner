    How to use
    • Add an item: type a name, its length (back-to-front), width (side-to-side) and height, then press Add to van. It goes into the first free spot it fits. Quick pick fills in typical sizes.
    • Move it: drag it with your mouse or finger. In the top view you move it around the floor. In the side view you can also drag it up or down.
    • Zoom: use the − / + buttons above the top view. When zoomed in, drag an empty part of the drawing to look around. On a phone, dragging an empty part of the drawing also scrolls the page.
    • Change it: tap an item, then edit its size, position, colour or name under Selected item. Use Rotate 90° to turn it.
    • Stacking: with Auto-stack on, an item dropped onto another one sits on top of it, and anything on top moves with the item underneath. You can also use Place on top of, or type a Sits at height. Tick Mounted for overhead cupboards or shelves.
    • Space left shows the free floor area (not counting the wheel arches) and the free space inside the van. Red notes warn if something hits a wheel arch, goes through a wall, is too tall or overlaps another item. Tap a note to select that item.
    • Saving: everything saves automatically on this device. Use Saved layouts to keep different versions, or export a file to back it up.
    • Picture / print: Save picture downloads a PNG of both views and your item list. Print lets you print or save as PDF.
    • Pricing: under the drawings (or press 💲 Pricing). Type your rates once – base fee, $ per m³, $ per km, and optionally handling per item, a minimum job price and GST. Every item in the van is listed with its volume. Put in the distance (km) and how many for each one (0 leaves it out). Add extra items that aren't in the drawing with their size. The price for each item and the job total update as you type. Copy quote gives you text to paste into a message. Print / save quote as PDF prints a clean quote.
    • Your van: sizes are under Van size & wheel arches. Measure the wheel arch height and put it in.
    • Keyboard (computer): arrow keys nudge the selected item (hold Shift for 10×), R rotate, D duplicate, Delete remove, Ctrl+Z undo.

    The back of the van (rear doors) is on the left of the drawings and the cab is on the right. The side view is seen from the driver's side.
